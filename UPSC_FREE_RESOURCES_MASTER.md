## Part 11: Zero-Cost Step-by-Step Study Sequence

1. **Months 1–3 — Foundation (Class 6–12 NCERTs + Syllabus + PYQs):**
   - Download the official syllabus & past 5 years' papers from [UPSC Previous Exam Details](https://www.upsc.gov.in/previous-exam-details/).
   - Complete **Class 6–12 NCERTs** subject-wise (History → Geography → Polity → Economy → Science/Ecology → Society → Fine Arts) using the direct official links in **Part 2**, paired with the free video playlists where concepts feel dense.
   - Read **The Hindu** or **The Indian Express (Explained)** + **PIB** for 45–60 minutes daily without making bulky notes initially.

2. **Months 4–7 — Standard Core + Optional Paper 1 & 2:**
   - Layer standard core books (**Part 3**) onto your NCERT base using free legal video companions (Atish Mathur / Bookstawa / Mrunal / Sudarshan Gurjar / PMF IAS) + **PRS Bill Track** + **2nd ARC Reports**.
   - Complete your Optional syllabus using **IGNOU eGyanKosh** + **e-PG Pathshala** + free YouTube lectures (**Part 10**).
   - Start writing 1–2 daily Mains answers on **InsightsIAS** / **ForumIAS** / **Drishti IAS** and compare against **Toppers' Answer Copies**.

3. **Months 8–10 — Prelims Intensive + CSAT + Current Affairs Consolidation:**
   - Switch to active MCQ solving: solve **2013–latest UPSC Prelims PYQs** twice (**Part 7**), practice daily free quizzes, and revise monthly current affairs + **Vision IAS PT 365** + **Economic Survey** + **Union Budget**.
   - Dedicate **1 hour/day to CSAT** using NCERT Maths (Class 8–10) + **UNGIST** / **Drishti IAS** free playlists + past CSAT papers (**Part 9**).

4. **Post-Prelims to Mains — Answer Writing, Ethics (GS-4), Essay & Mains 365:**
   - Practice timed 3-hour papers on official **UPSC Specimen QCAB** layout.
   - Revise **Ethics (GS-4)** case studies (2nd ARC 4th Report + Atish Mathur / ForumIAS free Ethics courses), **Essay** frameworks, and **Vision IAS Mains 365**.

5. **Post-Mains — Personality Test (DAF):**
   - Prepare every keyword of your DAF (Home State, Graduation, Hobby, Optional, Service Preference), watch **Sansad TV Perspective** & **Toppers' Mock Interviews**, and follow **AIR News / PIB** daily.
